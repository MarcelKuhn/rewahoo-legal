The privacy policy will be published after legal review, before Rewahoo launches. Until then this page only describes how this preview behaves today. It is not a legal document.

How this preview handles data today

  • • No third-party analytics or advertising trackers are loaded. Rewahoo’s own product analytics run only after you allow them under Cookies and storage, and only where the service has enabled them.
  • • Analytics events never contain your account, email address or any amount, and they are not linked to your account.
  • • Fonts and images are served from this website itself, not from third-party font or image services.
  • • Your privacy choice is stored in this browser’s local storage so we don’t ask again.
  • • If you sign in, the Rewahoo service sets a strictly necessary, HttpOnly session cookie. Sign-in is only possible where the service is connected.
  • • The hosting provider of this website processes technical access data, such as IP addresses, to deliver the pages.
  • • If you have an account, you can download a copy of your data and delete your account under Account → Privacy and data, in the app or on this website.
